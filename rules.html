Library Rules & Borrowing Policy

Borrowing limits differ for Students, Teachers, and Non-Teaching Staff.

Borrowing Rules by User Type

🎓 Students

Maximum Books 3
Borrowing Period 14 days
Renewal Allowed 1 time
Fine (after due date) ₹2 / day / book
Reference Books Not for regular borrowing

👨‍🏫 Teachers

Maximum Books 8
Borrowing Period 30 days
Renewal Allowed 2 times
Fine (after due date) ₹1 / day / book
Reference/Academic Subject to library policy

🏢 Non-Teaching Staff

Maximum Books 4
Borrowing Period 21 days
Renewal Allowed 1 time
Fine (after due date) ₹2 / day / book

Fine Calculation

Fines are calculated based on the number of overdue days and the user type:

Fine = Number of Overdue Days × Fine Rate per Day (for user type)

User Type Fine Rate Example (7 days overdue)
Student ₹2 per day per book 7 × ₹2 = ₹14
Teacher ₹1 per day per book 7 × ₹1 = ₹7
Non-Teaching Staff ₹2 per day per book 7 × ₹2 = ₹14

General Library Rules

  1. A valid college ID card is mandatory for library access and book borrowing.
  2. Books must be returned on or before the due date to avoid fines.
  3. Users with overdue books will not be allowed to borrow new books until the overdue books are returned.
  4. Library books should not be marked, highlighted, or damaged in any way. Damaged books must be replaced or compensated.
  5. Lost books must be reported immediately. The user will be required to pay the replacement cost of the book.
  6. Reference books and periodicals are for in-library use only and cannot be issued for home borrowing.
  7. The library reserves the right to recall any issued book at any time if needed.
  8. Silence must be maintained in the library premises.
  9. Personal belongings, food, and drinks are not allowed inside the library.
  10. Renewal requests must be made before or on the due date. Renewals are not guaranteed if the book is reserved by another user.
