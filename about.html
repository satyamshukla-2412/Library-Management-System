Our Library

The College Library is a central resource for students, teachers, and non-teaching staff. Established to support academic excellence, the library provides access to a wide collection of textbooks, reference books, journals, and digital resources across all engineering and science disciplines.

The library operates a computerized book issue and return system, allowing members to search for books, check availability, and manage their borrowing accounts through this online portal. The system tracks all transactions including issue dates, due dates, returns, and fines automatically.

  • Newspaper and magazine section
  • Photocopy facility
  • Internet access for academic research
  • Book bank facility for economically weaker students
